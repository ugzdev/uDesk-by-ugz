Privacy Policy for uDesk

Effective date: July 31, 2026
App name: uDesk
Package name: com.ugz.udesk

This Privacy Policy explains what information the uDesk Android application ("the app", "uDesk") collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. By installing or using uDesk, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. What uDesk does

uDesk is a remote control app that connects to a computer on the same local Wi-Fi network. It lets you control mouse input, view media playback information, adjust volume, view a list of apps and shortcuts, see wallpaper images, browse the web with a built-in browser, and check the weather. uDesk does not require an account or login, and it does not use a cloud server. All connections happen directly between your phone and the paired computer over your local network.

2. Information we collect

uDesk does not collect your name, email address, phone number, payment details, or any other information that could directly identify you. There is no account creation, login, or user profile in the app.

3. Permissions used by the app

Permission Purpose
INTERNET To connect to the paired computer over the local network and to call the OpenWeatherMap API
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE To check network connectivity and manage reconnect attempts
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE To detect the paired computer on the same Wi-Fi network
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Used only for the weather feature. Not used for any other purpose and not stored on any server.
WAKE_LOCK Keeps the screen or CPU awake during an active remote control session
WRITE_SETTINGS Allows the app to change certain device settings, such as screen brightness, when you request it

You can review or turn off any of these permissions at any time from your device settings under Apps > uDesk > Permissions. Turning off location will only disable the weather feature.

4. Local network communication

uDesk talks to the paired computer using a direct TCP connection (port 5050) and a UDP connection (port 5052), both on your local network. This connection is protected by a security token, so only your own paired computer can send or receive commands. No data from this connection passes through the internet, and no data is available to the developer of uDesk or to any third party.

5. Third party services

uDesk uses the OpenWeatherMap API (openweathermap.org) to show weather information. When you use this feature, your city name and an API key are sent to OpenWeatherMap. You can read their policy at openweathermap.org/privacy-policy.

uDesk does not use any advertising network, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, or crash reporting service, and does not collect an advertising ID. There are no ads in the app.

6. Where data is stored

uDesk does not have its own server or database. Any data that is saved (browsing history, language setting, other preferences) is stored only on your device, using Android's local storage. This data is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If Android's automatic backup is turned on for your device, this local data may be included in your own Google account backup, which is managed by Android, not by uDesk.

7. Data sharing

uDesk does not sell your data and does not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing. Data is only shared in these cases:

  • With your own paired computer, over your local network, so the app can work
  • With OpenWeatherMap, limited to a city name, for the weather feature
  • If required by law, such as a valid request from a court or government authority

8. Security

Commands sent to the paired computer require a security token, so unauthorized devices cannot control it. If the connection drops, the app retries with increasing delays instead of repeatedly hammering the connection. The built-in browser shows whether a page is using a secure (HTTPS) connection. No data leaves your local network except the OpenWeatherMap request described above. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

uDesk is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us using the information below and we will review and remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU), CCPA/CPRA (California), or KVKK (Turkey), including the right to know what data is processed, request access, request correction, request deletion, and object to processing. Because uDesk does not store personal data on any server, most of these actions can be done directly on your device: clear the app's browsing history in-app, clear app data from Settings > Apps > uDesk > Storage, or uninstall the app to remove all local data. For any other request, contact us using the details in section 12.
