Privacy Policy for uDesk

Last updated: July 31, 2026

UGZ Apps built the uDesk app as an Open Source app. This SERVICE is provided by UGZ Apps at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

uDesk operates strictly within your local area network (LAN) to facilitate remote control between your Android device and Windows PC. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, account credentials, or telemetry to external servers.

2. Permissions Required

  • Network / Wi-Fi Access: Required to establish TCP/UDP socket connections between the Android app and the PC server.
  • Camera / Device Sensors (if applicable): Any hardware tracking (e.g., gyroscope or camera input if implemented) is processed locally on the device in real-time and is never recorded or stored.

3. Security

Connections between the mobile app and the PC server are secured using a local Security Token generated by the desktop client. Since no data leaves your local network, your information remains private within your personal environment.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through our GitHub repository.
